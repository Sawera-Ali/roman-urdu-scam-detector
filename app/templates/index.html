Under the hood

A clear path
from text to signal.

Not a mysterious score. Four small steps turn an everyday SMS into a useful, human-readable decision.

01

Clean the text

Links, numbers, and punctuation are normalized so noise does not distract the model.

02

Understand the shorthand

Roman Urdu variants like "ap", "kren", and "sy" are brought closer together.

03

Map the language

TF-IDF features measure which words matter in this message and its context.

04

Compare the signal

Naive Bayes, SVM, or Logistic Regression turns those features into a result.

Built for the language
of real inboxes.

Most spam filters are tuned for English. But in Pakistan, a suspicious message might arrive in Roman Urdu — a mix of Urdu, English, shortcuts, and urgency that generic tools miss.

ScamGuard is a course-project proof of concept focused on that gap. It brings three classic machine-learning approaches into one calm place, so people can slow down before money, access, or trust leaves their hands.

Use the result as a second opinion, not a guarantee. ScamGuard is not connected to a bank, telecom provider, or government service.

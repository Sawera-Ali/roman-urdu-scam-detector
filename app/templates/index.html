    Inside the model Technical information +

    The model work evaluated Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, and SVM. This checker uses the saved Naive Bayes classifier with TF-IDF word and phrase features and the original Roman Urdu preprocessing pipeline.

    Confidence comes from the model's probability estimate. It has not been independently calibrated as a real-world safety score. The evaluation dataset is small and largely synthetic, so performance on new messages can differ.

    ABOUT SCAMGUARD

    A safer pause
    in your everyday.

    ScamGuard is designed around Roman Urdu SMS patterns. It helps you assess messages you choose to paste, with local machine learning and practical safety education.

    01

    Your messages stay out of a history.

    No account or database. Only scan result, confidence, ID, and time are saved locally; message text is never stored. This app doesn't read or intercept your SMS inbox.

    02

    Good judgment still matters.

    No classifier is perfect. Confirm financial and account requests through official channels. Never share OTPs, passwords, or PINs.
